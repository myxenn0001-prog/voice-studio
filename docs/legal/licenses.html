One item that needs your attention: GPL components

Fast mode's pronunciation step relies on espeak-ng and phonemizer, which, to our knowledge, are licensed under the GPL-3.0. Voice Studio's installers do not contain them: they are downloaded from the Python Package Index onto the user's own computer on first launch, which is the user's own use of those packages. Voice Studio's installers contain only our Apache-2.0 code and the small uv tool.

This reasoning stops applying if you (or anyone who forks the project) ever publish something that includes those packages, for example a pre-built Docker image or a fully offline installer. In that case you must comply with the GPL for those parts (include their license text and offer their source code), and you should ask a lawyer how the combined work is licensed.
