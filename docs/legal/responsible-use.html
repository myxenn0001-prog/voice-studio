Responsible use

Version 1.1

Voice Studio makes realistic synthetic speech, and its Clone mode can imitate a voice from a short recording. That is useful for creators, and it can be misused. Please use it the way you would want others to use it on you.

What we ask

  • Get permission. Only clone a voice that is yours, or that belongs to someone who has clearly agreed. The app asks you to confirm this each time you add a voice, and records that you did. Please mean it.
  • Do not deceive. Do not use it to impersonate a real person, to fake an endorsement, to commit fraud, to get past voice-based identity checks, to harass or threaten anyone, or to spread disinformation such as fake political statements.
  • Be open about synthetic audio where it matters: where people would reasonably expect a real human, where the law requires disclosure, or where a platform's rules ask for it. Many countries and platforms now require realistic AI-generated audio to be labelled.
  • Respect the law. Voice, likeness, publicity and privacy rights differ by country, and several places have specific laws about synthetic voices and deepfakes. You are responsible for how you use the audio.
  • Keep recordings private. Voice samples are personal data. Voice Studio stores them only on your computer, in the data/voices folder. Do not share someone else's sample without their permission.

What is built in

  • Clone mode needs a consent confirmation before a voice can be added. The confirmation is recorded with the voice, with the time and the version of the Terms.
  • Every file Voice Studio makes is marked as AI-generated. The file carries a note in its metadata ("AI-generated speech. Made with Voice Studio."), and audio is watermarked with an inaudible watermark: the Clone-mode model adds its own, and for other voices the app applies the same open-source watermarker when it is installed. Metadata is easy to strip and a watermark can be weakened by editing audio, so these marks help but cannot guarantee that synthetic audio is detected. Please do not remove them.
  • Nothing is uploaded: Voice Studio runs on your computer and has no server that can see your scripts or voices.
