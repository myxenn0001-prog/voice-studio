# Code of Conduct

Voice Studio is built by and for people from many countries, backgrounds and skill levels. We want everyone to feel welcome to ask questions, report problems and contribute.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent.
- Give specific, respectful feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome newcomers. Everyone started by not knowing.
- Respect other people's privacy. Do not post someone's voice recordings, personal details or private messages without their permission.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments, including about a person's identity, accent or language.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Using the project's spaces to promote or request help with impersonation, fraud, harassment or other misuse of voice cloning (see the Responsible use note).
- Sharing private information about others, or spamming.

## Where this applies

In the project's repository (issues, pull requests, discussions), on its website, and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock discussions, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, write to myofficialemail89@gmail.com. We will treat reports as confidential and
look into them fairly.
