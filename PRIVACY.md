# Privacy Notice

**Version 1.0 · Effective 31 October 2026**

**In short.** The Voice Studio app collects nothing. Your scripts, voices and audio stay on your computer. We run no servers that receive your data, and the app has no accounts, ads or tracking.

## 1. Who we are

Voice Studio is published by TechFlow ("we"). Contact: myofficialemail89@gmail.com. Because the app does not send your data to us, we do not hold personal data from your use of it.

## 2. The app (installed on your computer)

**What is stored, and where.** Everything is stored in the app's `data` folder on your own computer (see the README for its location):

| What | Details |
|---|---|
| Scripts | The text of each render is saved with that render, so the Past renders list can show it. |
| Audio and captions | The files you make. |
| Voice samples | Recordings you add, with a note of the time you confirmed permission. These can identify a person and may count as sensitive personal data. |
| Settings | Your choices, and the date you accepted the Terms of Use. |
| Logs | Technical messages in `data/server.log` and `setup.log`. In rare error cases they may include a fragment of text. |
| App-window profile | The window that opens the app keeps its own small browser profile in the `data` folder. It is not linked to any other browser. |

You can delete any of this at any time by deleting the files or the `data` folder. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

**What leaves your computer.** The app does not upload your content or send us usage data. It does contact other services to download software, and those services can see your IP address and basic request details under their own privacy policies:

| When | Service | What for |
|---|---|---|
| Setup (once) | GitHub | The setup tool (uv) and a copy of Python |
| Setup (once) | Python Package Index (pypi.org) and download.pytorch.org | The libraries the app runs on |
| Setup, or when you ask for a model | Hugging Face | The voice and caption models |
| Setup (once) | GitHub releases | A small language model used for English pronunciation |

After these downloads, the app works without internet.

## 3. The in-browser edition and our website

- **Website.** It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses under its own privacy statement. We add no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies of our own.
- **In-browser edition.** The voice runs inside your browser on your device. Your text is not sent to us or to anyone else. Your browser loads a script library from jsDelivr and a voice model from Hugging Face, which can see your IP address, and your browser stores the model in its cache so it does not download it again. Clear your browser data to remove it.

## 4. Voices and other people's data

Recordings of a person's voice are personal data. If you add someone else's voice, you are responsible for having their permission and a lawful reason to process it. Voice Studio keeps recordings only on your computer, and does not use them for anything except making the audio you ask for.

## 5. Children

The Services are not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect any data from them.

## 6. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data about your use of the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control your data directly on your computer. If you think we hold data about you, or you have a question about this notice, write to myofficialemail89@gmail.com.

## 7. Changes

We may update this notice. The current version, with its date, is available in the app and at https://myxenn0001-prog.github.io/voice-studio.
